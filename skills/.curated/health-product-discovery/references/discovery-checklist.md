# Discovery Checklist

Use this reference when product discovery needs deeper structure.

## Review Areas

- user type and decision-maker
- workflow before and after the proposed change
- safety, trust, privacy, and operational constraints
- interoperability or data dependencies
- regulatory or policy assumptions
- adoption barriers inside care delivery or healthcare operations

## Useful Outputs

- workflow summary
- risks and assumptions table
- open questions for subject matter experts
- proposed product scope and non-goals
