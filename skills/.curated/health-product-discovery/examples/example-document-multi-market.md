# Healthcare Product Discovery — Multi-Market Document Example

**Skill**: `health-product-discovery` (mode: document)
**Date**: 2026-03-31
**Selected overlays**: `us+eu`

## Context

Product: a remote heart-failure monitoring platform for outpatient cardiology teams and regional care-management programs.

Target markets:
- US integrated delivery networks and Medicare Advantage-aligned cardiology groups
- Germany and the Netherlands as initial EU markets through public and quasi-public care networks

## Shared Findings

- The clinical user is the care-management team, not the economic buyer.
- Success depends on workflow integration into existing cardiology review processes, not on a standalone dashboard.
- Evidence must show reduced admissions or better medication titration timing, not just patient engagement metrics.

## US Overlay Assumptions

- Buyer path likely runs through a health-system digital team, cardiology leadership, and value-based care owner.
- Business case depends on avoidable admissions, quality metrics, and downside-risk or shared-savings contracts.
- Procurement friction is likely to concentrate around EHR integration, security review, and pilot governance.

## EU Overlay Assumptions

- Deployment assumptions vary by member state; Germany and the Netherlands cannot be treated as the same buyer path.
- Public procurement and regional program sponsorship may matter more than department-level discretionary budgets.
- Localisation includes language, patient communication style, and country-specific care-pathway expectations.
- Reimbursement and HTA evidence thresholds may shape market access earlier than in the US motion.

## Risks And Open Questions

- Which market gets the first implementation, and which product assumptions are not portable?
- Does the product need cross-border data exchange support or only country-local deployment?
- Are MDR / AI Act questions triggered by any medication-adjustment recommendation logic?

## Recommended Next Step

Run two parallel validation tracks:
- a US buyer and incentive validation round with one value-based cardiology group
- a member-state-specific EU validation round focused on procurement, localisation, and reimbursement assumptions
