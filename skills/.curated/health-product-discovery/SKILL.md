# Healthcare Product Discovery

## Overview

Use this skill to shape early product decisions for healthcare software and digital health products before execution locks in poor assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the product goal, user type, and care or operations context.
2. Identify the workflow being changed, including handoffs and constraints.
3. Surface domain risks, assumptions, and evidence gaps.
4. Produce actionable outputs such as opportunity framing, requirements, risks, or decision memos.

## Constraints

- Distinguish user pain from clinical, compliance, and operational constraints.
- Make assumptions explicit when clinical or operational facts are unknown.
- Do not treat stakeholder wishes as validated workflow truth.

## Resources

- `references/discovery-checklist.md`: healthcare discovery prompts and review areas

## Output Contract

- Provide a concise problem framing, key stakeholders, workflow implications, risks, and next decisions.
