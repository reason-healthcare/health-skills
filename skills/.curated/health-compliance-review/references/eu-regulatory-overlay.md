# EU Regulatory Overlay

Use this overlay when repository evidence points to EU healthcare deployment, EU health-data handling, or EU product regulation.

## Primary Areas

- GDPR handling of special-category health data, controller/processor boundaries, data minimization, access, deletion, and transfer questions
- EHDS and health-data exchange expectations where primary-use or secondary-use flows are visible
- MDR / IVDR classification and intended-use questions for device-like software
- AI Act obligations where AI or ML influences clinical or operational decisions
- NIS2 cybersecurity expectations for operational healthcare systems and digital infrastructure

## High-Signal Evidence

- GDPR, DPA, DPO, data subject, lawful basis, special category data
- EHDS, MyHealth@EU, patient summary, ePrescription, cross-border health
- MDR, IVDR, CE marking, notified body, clinical evaluation
- AI Act, high-risk AI, human oversight, conformity assessment
- NIS2, essential entity, incident reporting, cybersecurity risk management

## Review Prompts

- Does the implementation show clear handling of health-data access, export, logging, analytics, and deletion under EU privacy expectations?
- Are controller/processor and vendor-boundary assumptions explicit or missing?
- Does the product act like medical-device software or diagnostics support that raises MDR/IVDR questions?
- Does AI-driven functionality appear in workflows that raise AI Act or human-oversight concerns?
- Does the system show operational or infrastructure characteristics that make NIS2-style cybersecurity obligations relevant?
